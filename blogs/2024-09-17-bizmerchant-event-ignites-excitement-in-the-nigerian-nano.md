PORT HARCOURT, NIGERIA - In a move that has sent ripples of excitement through the Nigerian Nano community, Madora.io and other Nano representatives have once again joined forces to host the latest edition of the 'BizMerchant 2.0’ event. This event, focused on driving innovation, awareness, and adoption of cutting-edge merchant solutions, is fast becoming a cornerstone for aspiring Nigerian entrepreneurs in the technology space.
